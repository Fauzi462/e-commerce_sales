\# E-Commerce Analysis



\## 📌 Project Overview

The company is analyzing e-commerce product sales for the month of April. this project examines sales trends, discounts, and profits with the goal of maximizing the company's revenue. The findings from this analysis are expected to provide insights for stakeholders and assist them in maximizing sales profits in the coming months.



\## 🛠️ Tech Stack \& Libraries

• Microsoft Excel

• Python -Pandas

• Google Colab

• Power BI



\## 📈 Key Insight \& Visualization



Dashboard



* The Marketplace with the highest total transaction value is Shopee, which reached 185 million rupiah in a single month
* Meanwhile, the location with the highest total transaction value was the Greater Jakarta area (Jabodetabek), with a total transaction value of 151 million rupiah over the course of one month.
* Based on sales trends, total transactions experienced a significant decline after the 6th; prior to the 6th, daily transactions ranged between 30 and 40 million rupiah, whereas after the 6th, this figure dropped to approximately 10 million rupiah per day.
* The product with the highest sales volume was SP002 (slip-on sandals), with 492 units sold in one month.
* The product that generated the highest total transaction value was SP001 (sneakers), with a total transaction value of 67 million rupiah over the course of one month.
* The product with the highest production cost was PP003 (denim jacket), with total production costs reaching 58 million rupiah for the month.



Dashboard



* Regarding discount costs, from the 1st to the 6th of each month, these costs range between 4 and 5 million rupiah per day. From the 7th to the 15th, there are no discount costs at all, while from the 16th to the 30th, discount costs reappear, ranging from 1 to 2 million rupiah per day.
* There are two types of discounts: "twin-date" discounts (for example, the 4.4 promotion) and Eid (Lebaran) discounts. The total cost for twin-date discounts reaches 27 million rupiah in a single month, which is higher than the 24 million rupiah spent on Eid discounts during the same period.
* Shopee is the marketplace with the highest expenditure on discounts, totaling 24 million rupiah in one month.
* Shopee also leads in sales volume, with 423 products sold throughout the month.
* Furthermore, Shopee recorded the highest total profit, reaching 160 million rupiah in a single month.
* Although Shopee achieved the highest total profit, Tokopedia holds the highest net profit margin, at 12.72%.



\## 💡 Recommendations



* Maximize sales on Shopee while expanding reach across other marketplaces, given that Shopee currently generates the highest total transactions, sales volume, and profit.
* Focus on consumers in the Greater Jakarta area (Jabodetabek: Jakarta, Bogor, Depok, Tangerang, and Bekasi) while working to boost sales in other regions, as Jabodetabek accounts for the highest number of transactions.
* There is no significant disparity in sales volume across products; each item has sold between 300 and 500 units. Nevertheless, the company should strive to increase sales for products with the lowest volume—specifically PW001 (dresses), PW003 (kaftans), and PP002 (shorts).
* Product PP003 (denim jackets) incurs the highest production costs, yet its transaction count remains lower than that of SP001 (sneakers). Furthermore, SP001 (sneakers) currently leads in total transactions, even though its unit sales volume is lower than that of several other products. Consequently, the company should aim to reduce the production costs of PP003 (denim jackets) and continue to drive sales of SP001 (sneakers) to maximize profit.
* Sales trends appear to be heavily influenced by discounts or promotions; sales volume tends to rise during promotional periods and decline when such offers are unavailable. This should be managed to ensure that consumers do not become overly reliant on discounts or promotions, but rather make purchases based on product quality and their trust in the brand.
* There are two types of discounts: "double-date" promotions (e.g., the 4.4 sale) and Lebaran (Eid) promotions. Of the two, double-date promotions generate a higher average daily transaction value. The total transaction volume during double-date promotions is significantly greater than that of Lebaran promotions, even though the former lasts only 6 days—a much shorter duration than the 15-day Lebaran promotional period. Therefore, companies should leverage these double-date events when they need to achieve high sales volume within a relatively short timeframe.
* Although Shopee leads in terms of total transactions and sales volume, its net profit margin remains lower than that of Tokopedia—despite the fact that Shopee incurs the highest costs related to discounts. This is likely because the products sold on Shopee tend to have smaller margins, whereas products on Tokopedia generally offer larger margins. Consequently, to maximize profits, companies should prioritize high-margin products when selling on Shopee.
